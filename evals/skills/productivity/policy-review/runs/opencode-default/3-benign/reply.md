**Bottom line**
Fernway Weather is a weather app with an ordinary privacy policy. It keeps data in Australia, sets retention periods, and commits to breach notification. Nothing here is a show stopper, and the gaps are procedural.

**What to know**
1. **No automated decisions section** (S6, Worth knowing). The policy has no section on decisions made by a program. "To show a forecast for your suburb, to send an alert you asked for, and to keep the service working." (How we use it). Alerts are generated automatically, so ask what a program decides and how a person reviews it.
2. **Change notice by posting only** (S9, Worth knowing). "We post the current policy on this page." (Changes). You are not told before a material change takes effect, so the terms can move without notice. Keep a dated copy of the version you accepted.
3. **No named privacy officer** (S11, Worth knowing). "You can access or correct your information by emailing privacy@fernway.example." (Your rights). The contact is a role address with no named owner and no complaint response time. Keep the date of any request.

**What is normal**
G1 to G6 all pass. S1, S2, S3, S4, S5, S7, S8, and S10 carry nothing material.

**What you can do**
