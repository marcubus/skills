**Bottom line**
Lumen Ledger Pty Ltd runs an Australian transaction ledger. The policy trains models on your uploads, shares data with partners for their own marketing, and sets no retention limit, no breach notice, and no minors position. Nothing here is a show stopper.

**What to know**
1. **No minors position** (G6, High attention). No section addresses children or household data (Section 2, Information we collect). It takes transaction records, device identifiers and approximate location, and nothing limits a child's data or its advertising use. Leave household fields empty.
2. **Breach silence** (G5, High attention). "We use encryption in transit, access controls, and monitoring. We cannot guarantee that our security measures will prevent every unauthorised access." (Section 7, Security). Measures are named, notification is not, so a breach reaches you late. Keep your contact address current.
3. **Partners marketing with your data** (G1, S4, High attention). "We may also share your information with trusted business partners, who may use it for their own marketing purposes." (Section 4, Sharing). Trusted sets no limit and no opt-out is offered, so partners can use your spending data for themselves. Ask for the partner list.
4. **Open-ended overseas transfer** (G3, High attention). "Your information may be transferred to countries where we or our providers operate." (Section 8, Overseas transfers). No countries, no mechanism, no accountability, so you cannot tell where your records sit. Ask which countries hold them.
5. **Training on your uploads** (G2, S6, High attention). "We may also use your information, including the content you upload, to develop and improve our services and to train our machine learning models." (Section 3, How we use your information). No opt-in and no automated-decision section. Your ledgers can train their models. Object in writing.
6. **Retention with no maximum** (G4, High attention). "We retain information for as long as necessary for our legitimate business purposes." (Section 6, Retention). No period and no destruction commitment, so the permission is unbounded. Ask for the period.
7. **Licence over your content, plus the smaller gaps** (S3, S1, S2, S8, S9, High attention). "You grant us a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, sublicensable licence to use, reproduce, adapt, and commercially exploit the content you upload." (Section 5, Your content). Location and device identifiers carry no stated purpose, sensitive details no consent, marketing no withdrawal route or change notice. Keep originals elsewhere.

**What is normal**
S5 names real measures, S7 gives a route and 30 days, S10 numbering holds, S11 names a contact and the OAIC path. Every gate applies to this document type.
