# Fernway Weather Privacy Policy

Effective 12 January 2026.

## Information we collect

Your suburb, the locations you save, and the device type you use. We do not collect precise location unless you turn on storm alerts.

## How we use it

To show a forecast for your suburb, to send an alert you asked for, and to keep the service working. We do not use your information to train models.

## Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information for third-party marketing. Our forecast provider processes suburb names on our behalf under contract.

## Overseas

Data is hosted in Sydney, Australia, with our provider Amazon Web Services. No data leaves Australia.

## Retention

We delete account data 30 days after you close your account. Alert history is deleted after 12 months.

## Security and breaches

We use encryption in transit and at rest. If a breach is likely to result in serious harm, we will notify affected users and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

## Your rights

You can access or correct your information by emailing privacy@fernway.example. We respond within 30 days. If we refuse a correction, you can ask us to attach a statement to your record. You can complain to us, and then to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

## Changes

We post the current policy on this page.
