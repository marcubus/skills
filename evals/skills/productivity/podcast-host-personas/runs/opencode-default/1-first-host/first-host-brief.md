# Brief: first host, new show

Free text, as an operator would send it.

---

New show, single host, weekly. Working title is *Object Lessons*, the history of everyday objects, one object an episode. Spoon, zip, pencil, that sort of thing.

The host is a woman in her fifties, Northern Irish, called Maeve Sweeney. Warm and impatient, in that order. She has presented radio before and she is not precious about it. No idea what her emotional range should be. Pick whatever suits a solo presenter who has to carry forty minutes alone.

She records at home, in the small carpeted spare room, on a decent dynamic mic. Nothing fancy.

I do not want her to sound like a narrator reading a script. She should sound like someone who has handled the objects and has opinions about them.
