# bridge-report-podcast-script (kokoro-tone)

[voice:host_a] The bridge failed on its first day. Not in a storm. Not under a crowd, or a truck, or a flood. In a light breeze, on a Tuesday, while the market below it was open.

[pause:600ms]

[voice:host_b] And nobody was hurt?

[voice:host_a] Nobody was hurt. The deck moved, the council closed it, and the market went home.

[pause:400ms]

[voice:host_b] So what did the model get wrong?

[voice:host_a] [rate:0.95] It assumed a crowd that keeps walking. [sense:bass=base] The market has a bass player under the eastern arch, and people stop to listen.

[pause:400ms]

[voice:host_b] They stop. The model assumed they move.

[voice:host_a] The model assumed they move. Nine thousand one hundred and forty people came through on day one. The design assumed three thousand nine hundred and thirteen.

[pause:600ms]

[voice:host_b] That is not a rounding error.

[voice:host_a] [emotion:sad] No. It is a different bridge.

[pause:400ms]

[voice:host_b] Who signed it?

[voice:host_a] Lachie Fraser. He says he got the traffic figure after the model was fixed. The review found the figure went around three weeks before he signed.

[pause:400ms]

[voice:host_b] Did he sign the revision?

[voice:host_a] He did not. He said the revision changed the assumption without changing the drawing.

[pause:600ms]

[voice:host_b] And the second staircase?

[voice:host_a] [rate:1.05] Added during construction. Never in the scope. Never checked again. The review calls it the only conservative part of the structure.

[pause:400ms]

[voice:host_b] Which is the part that gets me.

[voice:host_a] [emotion:warm] Mine too. Somebody built the honest staircase by accident.

[pause:1000ms]

[voice:host_a] [rate:1.0] The bridge cost thirty one million. The review cost two point four. The fix is six point eight, and the market loses a season either way.

[pause:400ms]

[voice:host_b] What does the review actually recommend?

[voice:host_a] Replace the bearing. Load test the span. One record, one version of each calculation. And a crowd plan for any bridge over a market.

[pause:600ms]

[voice:host_b] Will they do it?

[voice:host_a] [rate:0.95] They will do the bearing. The record is the hard part.

[pause:600ms]

[voice:host_a] [rate:0.9] One line. The bridge was checked against a model, and the model was wrong. Ask who checked the model before you ask who signed the drawing.
